Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions

What types of peptides does QELVION manufacture?+
We manufacture lyophilized research-grade peptides across metabolic, regenerative, dermal and longevity research areas — flagship catalog lines include BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, AHK-Cu, NAD+ and Epithalon — together with custom peptides, modified analogs, precision research blends and private-label programs. All products are supplied as lyophilized powders in sterile vials for laboratory use.
What purity and quality control can I expect?+
Typical purity is ≥99%, verified by HPLC and mass spectrometry. Each batch is released with a Certificate of Analysis covering peptide content, identity, residual solvents and more — and third-party laboratory verification is available on request.
Do you provide documentation with every order?+
Yes. Every shipment includes the batch COA, MSDS and technical datasheets, with HPLC chromatograms and full traceability records available on request.
Can you synthesize custom or modified peptides?+
Absolutely. We accept confidential custom-synthesis projects from milligram research scale to kilogram production: sequence modifications, D-amino acids, cyclization, PEGylation, labeling and more. NDAs are welcome before any technical discussion.
What are your minimum order quantities?+
MOQs are flexible. Research institutions can start with small first orders to evaluate quality, purity documentation and service, while distributors and production partners benefit from dedicated volume pricing and private-label programs.
Do you ship internationally?+
Yes — we serve customers in 35+ countries and regions with insulated, temperature-monitored cold-chain shipping and end-to-end tracking through trusted international carriers. Standard catalog orders typically dispatch within 3 working days.
Why do your products state “research use only”?+
QELVION products are research and development reagents manufactured for laboratory study. They are not medicines, dietary supplements or cosmetics, are not intended for human or veterinary use, and are not for self-administration. We do not provide medical advice of any kind — research into biological mechanisms should always be conducted under appropriate ethical and regulatory oversight.
How do I start an order or partnership?+
Send us a request through the quote form or email — tell us your research focus or catalog list, and we will reply within one business day with availability, documentation samples and a formal quotation.
