ORDER CONFIRMATION EMAIL — NEUTRAL TEMPLATE (paste into Fastmail)
=================================================================
WHY: drug names in the subject/body (Retatrutide, Semax, Selank…) are
the single biggest spam trigger, together with markdown asterisks and
template leftovers such as "**Subject:**". Use internal codes instead.

SUBJECT (keep short, no product names, no asterisks)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Order confirmation · {ORDER_ID} | Qelvion Biotech

BODY (plain text, no asterisks, no attachments)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Hello {FIRST_NAME},

Thank you for your order with Qelvion Biotech. Please review the summary below.

Order {ORDER_ID}
Items
  {CODE} · {QTY} box ({VIALS} vials) — ${PRICE}
  {CODE} · {QTY} box ({VIALS} vials) — ${PRICE}
  {CODE} · {QTY} box ({VIALS} vials) — ${PRICE}
Total: ${TOTAL} (shipping included)

Ship to
  {ADDRESS_LINE_1}
  {CITY}, {STATE} {ZIP}, {COUNTRY}

Payment: received

Your order has been passed to our warehouse and is being prepared for
shipment. Once it has been dispatched we will send you the tracking
number so you can follow the delivery.

If any of the information above is incorrect, please let us know as soon
as possible so we can update your order before it leaves our facility.

Best regards,
{SALES_NAME} — Qelvion Biotech
orders@qelvionbiotech.com · https://qelvionbiotech.com

-----------------------------------------------------------------
RULES THAT KEEP IT OUT OF SPAM
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Never put the word "Subject:" or ** asterisks ** inside the subject or body.
2. No PDF/attachments — link to a page instead if you must share a document.
3. Use product CODES (see product-codes.csv) instead of full drug names.
4. Keep the total number of links low (0–2). No URL shorteners (bit.ly etc.).
5. Send replies inside the customer's existing thread whenever possible.
6. Test with DUMMY data only (never a real customer's name/address).
7. Ask the customer once to add orders@qelvionbiotech.com to their contacts
   and to press "Not spam" if it lands in the spam folder.
